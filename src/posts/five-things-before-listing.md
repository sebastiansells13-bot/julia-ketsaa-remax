---
title: 5 Things to Know Before Listing Your Home
description: A few small fixes and the right timing can meaningfully change your sale price.
date: 2026-02-03
tags: seller-tips
---
1. **Small fixes pay off.** A fresh coat of paint and fixing that one sticky door
   matter more to buyers than you'd think.
2. **Price it right the first time.** Overpricing and cutting later almost always
   nets less than pricing accurately from day one.
3. **Photos make the first impression.** Most buyers decide whether to book a showing
   from photos alone — this is not the place to cut corners.
4. **Timing matters, but less than you'd guess.** A well-priced, well-presented home
   sells in most seasons.
5. **A pre-listing inspection can save you a renegotiation later.** Knowing what's
   coming lets you get ahead of it.
