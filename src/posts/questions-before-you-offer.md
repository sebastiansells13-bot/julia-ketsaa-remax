title: Questions to Ask Before You Make an Offer
description: A few things worth checking before you put pen to paper on a home.

tags: buyer-tips
---
Falling for a house is easy. Before you write an offer, it's worth slowing down
for a few questions:

1. **Why is it listed?** A quick move, a job relocation, an estate sale — the
   reason can shape how flexible a seller is on price or timeline.
2. **How long has it been on the market?** A home that's sat for a while may
   have more room to negotiate than one that just listed.
3. **What's not included?** Appliances, fixtures, and window treatments aren't
   automatically part of the sale — worth confirming in writing.
4. **What do the seller's disclosures say?** Read them closely, and ask about
   anything that isn't clear before, not after, you're under contract.
5. **What will inspection likely turn up?** No home is perfect. Knowing the
   probable issues going in helps you write a stronger, more realistic offer.

None of this should slow you down for long — it just means your offer goes in
with your eyes open. Happy to walk through any of it with you before you write one.
