Talavera sits east of the city near the foothills of the Organ Mountains, and it's where people look when they want more room. Homesites here are generally much larger than in town, with wide views back across the Mesilla Valley and the mountains close behind you.

The draw is space and access to the outdoors: trails in Organ Mountains-Desert Peaks National Monument are a short drive away.

Larger, more rural lots come with their own questions: water, septic, road maintenance, and how far you'll drive for groceries and school. Before you make an offer, confirm how the specific property handles each of those. I can help you ask the right questions.
