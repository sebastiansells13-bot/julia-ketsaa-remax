New Mexico State University's main campus sits on the south side of Las Cruces, and the neighborhoods around it have a character all their own. Founded in 1888, NMSU is New Mexico's oldest public university, and it shapes daily life nearby: game days, concerts, lectures, and the rhythm of the school year.

Housing near campus is a mix of established single-family homes, condos, and properties that are often rented out. It's a common choice if you work at the university and want a short commute, if you're buying a place for a student, or if you're looking for a rental investment.
