Sonoma Ranch is one of the best-known master-planned areas in Las Cruces. It spreads across the East Mesa and climbs gently toward the foothills of the Organ Mountains, so many streets get a mountain view. Most of the homes are newer construction, with shopping and restaurants along the main roads nearby.

At its center is Sonoma Ranch Golf Club, an 18-hole public course that opened in 2000, so you don't need a club membership to play.

Sonoma Ranch is made up of several subdivisions built over time, and homeowners association rules and fees vary from one to the next. Ask for the HOA documents early for any home you're considering, and I'll help you compare them.
