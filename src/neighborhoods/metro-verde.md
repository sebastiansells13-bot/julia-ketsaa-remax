Metro Verde is a good picture of how Las Cruces is growing. It's a large master-planned community on the city's northeast side, built around Red Hawk Golf Club, with new phases of homes still underway.

One thing sets it apart: the homes are built all-electric, without natural gas lines. That means electric heating, water heating, and cooking, which is worth knowing if your heart is set on a gas stove.

Buying new construction has its own process: choosing a lot and floor plan, reviewing the builder's contract, and walking the home before closing. Having your own agent through it means someone is looking out for you, not the builder.
